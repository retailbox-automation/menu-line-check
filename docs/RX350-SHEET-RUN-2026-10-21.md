# A real service sheet, run live (2026-10-21)

The owner's own printed sheet from the 20,000-mile service of a 2024 RX 350 (17.10.2026), photographed
and redacted (VIN, dealer, service advisor, logos, product brands, lender and form code removed). Two
sheets from the same visit:

- the dealer's recommended sheet, TOTAL $801.05 (`tests/fixtures/lexus_sheet_801_vision.json`);
- the sheet printed after the owner declined four lines, TOTAL $391.38, with those four under a
  "Declined" header (`tests/fixtures/lexus_sheet_391_vision.json`).

Neither sheet prices a single line: nine bullet lines, then Menu Total, Shop Charges, Tax, Fees, TOTAL.
The OCR is Apple Vision on the Mac (free), written in the shape of an Amazon Textract
`DetectDocumentText` response by `scripts/vision_to_textract.swift`; no Textract call was made.

## The run

Two runs, 2026-10-21 11:12:12Z to 11:12:22Z, Decider 2B on our reference server (MPS, one request at a
time) through `127.0.0.1:18299`, NHTSA live on the first run and from the disk cache on the second,
no Bedrock (template advisor questions, no LLM picker), default thresholds. Cost $0.

```bash
menu-check --ocr-json tests/fixtures/lexus_sheet_801_vision.json \
  --after tests/fixtures/lexus_sheet_391_vision.json \
  --vehicle "2024 Lexus RX 350 20000" --decider-url http://127.0.0.1:18299 \
  --nhtsa-cache out/nhtsa-cache --ledger out/ledger-lexus-1.jsonl --json out/report-lexus-1.json
```

The make is there because the NHTSA API needs it. Printed tables: `out/run-lexus-1.txt`,
`out/run-lexus-2.txt` (not committed; `out/` is ignored).

**Deterministic:** both runs gave the same route, tool, Decider choice, confidence, P(safety) and
verdict on all 11 lines; only the milliseconds differ.

- Decider's top tool is one the label accepts on **10 of 11** lines; the miss is the crankcase cleaning

- Safety: wheel alignment (P(safety) 0.89) went to the owner. Tire rotation was flagged too (0.78)

  safety-flagged line automatically" guards `decline_addon`, not the schedule lookup.

## Recalls card

As printed in run 1, with the vehicle shown without the make, the note wrapped and the one-line
summaries moved below:

```
Recalls · 2024 RX 350 · NHTSA, by model, not by VIN
  NHTSA returns no campaign for model "RX 350"; model family "RX" has 4, of which 3 name the RX 350 in
  their summary (by model, not by VIN).
  family "RX": kept 24V482000, 25V059000, 26V162000; not naming the RX 350: 25V744000
  24V482000  2024-06-27  SEATS:FRONT ASSEMBLY:HEAD RESTRAINT
  25V059000  2025-02-06  SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:WEBBING
  26V162000  2026-03-18  BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
  Ask: "Are there open recalls on my VIN?"
```

One-line summaries in the report: "The head restraints on the driver and front passenger seats may be
inadvertently removed without pressing the lock release button." · "The second-row center seat belt
webbing may be damaged." · "The rearview camera image may not display when the vehicle is placed in
reverse." 25V744000 (rearview camera freeze, Panoramic View Monitor) is not kept because its summary
says "Please see the recall report for a complete list of models" instead of naming the RX 350.

Found on the live API during this run: for model "RX 350" the recalls API answers **HTTP 400** with an
ordinary body `{"Count":0,"Message":"Results returned successfully","results":[]}` (also for a made-up
model), while "RX" answers 200 with four campaigns. The client now reads that 400 as "no campaigns";
before the fix the first live run printed "Recall lookup failed (http)". A naive agent would have told
the owner either "no recalls" or "lookup failed"; three campaigns name this car's model.

## Against the owner's real declines

The owner declined four lines on the second sheet; matched to the first sheet by normalised text
(the cabin filter line reads "Furgicide" on one photo and "Fungicide" on the other):

| Owner declined | Agent said |
|---|---|
| 2 Premium engine oil conditioner | decline (decline_addon, 0.87) |
| 4 Cabin filter + sanitizer | decline (check_schedule: not due until 30,000 mi in the illustrative table) |
| 8 Crankcase cleaning service | ask (Decider check_schedule at 0.39, below threshold) |
| 9 A/C evaporator cleaning | ask (Decider decline_addon at 0.62, below the 0.8 decline threshold) |

- The agent said decline on **2 of the 4** lines the owner declined; the other two became questions
  for the advisor, not keeps.
- The agent said decline on **2 lines the owner kept**: engine oil and tire rotation. Both come from the
  illustrative schedule (every 7,500 mi, so not due until 22,500 mi), not from Decider, which picked
  `check_schedule` correctly on both. That table is not the maker's schedule.
- The lines the owner kept otherwise got keep (inspection), ask (protection plan) and your call
  (alignment).

## What this does not show

- Eleven lines from one visit are an example, not an accuracy measure. The measure stays the 103
  labelled synthetic lines in the README.
- The labels are our judgement from the line text (two lines accept two tools); the owner's declines
  are one owner's choices on one day, not ground truth for what a line "is".
- The maintenance table is illustrative and generic. Every schedule verdict above (oil, rotation,
  cabin filter not due; inspection due) comes from that table, not from the maker's schedule for this car.
- The recalls card lists campaigns for the model and year. Whether any is open on this car needs the
  VIN, which is why the card only asks.
- OCR is Apple Vision on redacted photos, not Textract on the original sheet; redaction split some
  printed rows into two OCR blocks, which the parser joins back.
- No LLM picker and no Bedrock writer ran: lines 6, 8 and 9 would go to the picker in a paid run.
